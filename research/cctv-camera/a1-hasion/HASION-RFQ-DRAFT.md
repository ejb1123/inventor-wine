Subject: X6Z7 housing sample and mechanical integration information — custom 4K60 camera

Hello Hasion team,

We are developing a custom outdoor 3840 x 2160 / 60 fps camera and are evaluating
your X6Z7 housing, preferably the white-body/black-front version shown in your
catalog. We intend to source the enclosure from Hasion and have our electronics
and internal mounting parts manufactured separately.

The camera targets a Sony IMX585-class MIPI module, PoE Ethernet, Wi-Fi,
Bluetooth, USB-C power/service, Linux and local AI. A future variant adds
LTE/5G. Our proposed compute module is Forlinx FET3588J-C (68 x 50 mm), and
the cellular candidate is Quectel RM520N (52 x 30 x 2.3 mm excluding socket,
antenna connectors and cables). A FRAMOS sensor-board candidate is 26.5 x
26.5 mm. These are candidate components, not a frozen design.

We read the X6Z7 catalog as 107.5 mm body length, 75.2 mm external front width,
and 180.5 mm overall including mount. Please correct any misinterpretation.
The 38/42 mm sensor-board support does not tell us the usable electronics bay.
Our simple flat SoM/modem layouts require either 68 x 80 mm or 120 x 50 mm,
so a stacked arrangement or another housing appears necessary. We cannot
approve fit from external dimensions.

Please provide:

1. Exact X6Z7 variants/part numbers and sample availability. Please quote one and
   two samples, plus indicative 100- and 1,000-unit pricing, MOQ, lead times,
   customization charges and what is included. These are quote quantities only.
2. Internal STEP files and dimensioned PDF drawings: usable cavity, sensor and
   processor board mounts, screw sizes/patterns, board-stack spacing, maximum
   component heights, lens diameter/length, optical axis and window clear aperture.
   Please distinguish the 38/42 mm sensor-board mounting provision from space
   available for other boards.
   Include cavity cross-sections at several positions, all screw-boss and
   gasket intrusions, board insertion/removal path, service-cover clearance,
   wall thickness and maximum component/stack heights. Can the housing accept
   a 68 x 50 mm SoM plus custom carrier and cooling? A 74 x 56 mm plan rectangle
   is our initial screening allowance only; total assembled height is TBD.
3. Front-window and lens-holder options for this sensor size, including IR-cut
   provisions and optical separation if infrared illumination is added later.
4. Antenna opening/bulkhead options and a weather-sealed USB-C service solution.
   Can the existing service compartment support USB-C, with access for a cable,
   while retaining the stated ingress protection when closed?
5. Ethernet cable sealing/strain relief and routing through the mount. Can an
   assembled connector pass through, or must the cable be terminated afterward?
6. Housing materials, finish, operating-temperature guidance, vent options,
   internal heat-spreader attachment points, and relevant ingress-test evidence
   for the exact chosen configuration. Electronics dissipation is not final;
   please share any tested heat-load information or restrictions.
7. Whether the supplied assembly includes glass, gaskets, mount, sunshield,
   fasteners, cable seals and service-cover tether, plus available spare seals.
8. Please evaluate two configurations separately: (A) PoE/Wi-Fi/Bluetooth and
   (B) the same camera plus LTE/5G modem. Provide space for SIM access, modem
   socket/standoff, antenna cable bend radii and sealed RF bulkheads. Identify
   plastic RF-transparent regions versus metal sections. Antenna count and
   band coverage are not frozen; please send available opening locations.
9. Can you supply an adapter for the 26.5 mm sensor board while maintaining the
   lens optical axis? Please provide compatible lens-holder/IR-cut envelopes
   for a 1/1.2-type sensor. Lens and field of view are still being selected.
10. Our target ambient is -20 to +50 C. Please provide thermal attachment
    drawings and any tested heat-load limits under sun exposure. No heat-load
    value is finalized; we need to establish whether active cooling or a
    larger housing is necessary. Describe USB-C sealing both with the service
    cover closed and with a cable attached; these are different conditions.

If X6Z7 cannot accommodate this arrangement, please suggest the closest compact
bullet housing with more board-stack space and provide the same drawings.
Please also identify any compatible sealed electronics base/junction box,
with internal dimensions, if the modem/power electronics must be separated.
We would design and validate the interconnect separately.
Do not start tooling, customization or production on this inquiry.

Thank you.
